About Fluffistry

Character-first, always.

Fluffistry began as a way to solve a problem we kept running into as commissioners ourselves: most studios treat a character like a product spec, not an identity. We wanted a studio that starts from who the character is, and lets every material and design choice follow from that.

Today the studio handles reference sheets, character design, fursuits and illustration under one roof, so a character stays coherent no matter which service it moves through.

Our Approach

Slow enough to get it right, fast enough to matter.

Every commission moves through the same disciplined process — concept, design, creation, delivery — regardless of size. We limit how many projects we take on at once so each one gets real attention, not a rushed pass between bigger jobs.

Materials and techniques are chosen per character, not applied from a single default. A short-furred character and a heavily marked one are approached differently, because they should be.
