About Fluffistry

A studio built around a single idea: your character deserves to exist properly.

Fluffistry is a creative studio dedicated to turning original characters into memorable visual identities — on paper, in reference sheets, and in wearable form. Every project starts with the character, not the template.

We work with fursuit performers, illustrators, streamers and collectors who want their character handled with genuine craft rather than rushed through a queue.

Our Process

From first idea to finished character, in four stages.

01

Concept

We talk through your character, references, and how the piece will be used, then confirm scope and timeline.

02

Design

Sketches and design decisions are shared for approval before any final materials or artwork are produced.

03

Creation

Your approved design is built or illustrated in full, with progress updates at each meaningful milestone.

04

Final Delivery

Finished files or the finished suit are delivered, with a short revision window included in every package.

Good to know

Common questions before you commission.

How long does a commission take?

Reference sheets typically take 2–4 weeks; fursuits range from 6–14 weeks depending on complexity and queue position.

How does payment work?

A deposit secures your queue slot, with the remaining balance due in agreed stages before final delivery.

Can I request revisions?

Yes — every package includes a defined revision window at the design stage, before final production begins.
