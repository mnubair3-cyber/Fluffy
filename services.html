Services

Four disciplines, handled under one roof.

Every service below can stand alone or be combined, so your character stays consistent from the first sketch to the finished piece.

Custom Fursuits
01 — Custom Fursuits

Full and partial suits, built to fit and to move.

Every suit is patterned to your measurements and your character's proportions, then constructed with materials chosen for how the character will actually be worn and performed in.

What's included: pattern development, follow-along or premade base fur matching, sculpted or foam-based head construction, hand and foot paws, and a fitting check before final assembly.

Suitable for: performers, streamers and collectors who want a suit built specifically around their character rather than adapted from an existing base.

Request a Fursuit Quote
02 — Reference Sheets

A single source of truth for your character.

Reference sheets lay out your character's proportions, markings, palette and key details clearly enough that any artist — or fursuit maker — can work from them accurately.

What's included: front and side views, a colour palette with hex codes, close-ups of markings or accessories, and a short written description of personality-relevant details.

Suitable for: anyone who wants their character to stay consistent across multiple artists, platforms or future commissions.

Character Design
03 — Fursona / Character Design

Starting from a blank page, together.

For clients without an existing character, we design one from the ground up — species, silhouette, palette and personality — through a short collaborative back-and-forth rather than a single guess.

What's included: a species and concept discussion, 2–3 initial design directions, one round of refinement on your chosen direction, and a finished character turnaround.

Suitable for: newcomers to the fandom, or anyone reworking an existing character that no longer fits.

Start Designing a Character
04 — Custom Artwork

Illustration suited to how it will be used.

From a single character portrait to a full illustrated scene, artwork is rendered in a style and format chosen for its purpose — social media, print, or a personal keepsake.

What's included: a sketch for approval, one round of revisions, and final high-resolution files delivered in the format you need.

Suitable for: anniversaries, badges, prints, or simply wanting to see your character in a new scene.
